Who We Are
A disciplined real estate investment company specializing in affordable housing.

Aligned Compass Holdings is a private real estate investment company focused on acquiring, improving, and operating affordable housing assets in carefully selected markets. We prioritize conservative underwriting, practical residential properties, and long-term operational stewardship.

What We Do

A clear, repeatable approach to residential housing assets

We identify, evaluate, and operate properties against consistent standards — from acquisition through long-term ownership.

01

Acquire

We source and evaluate residential properties against defined standards for market strength, rent support, acquisition basis, condition, and operational feasibility.

02

Improve

We prioritize durable, practical improvements that support safety, inspection readiness, tenant stability, and long-term asset performance.

03

Operate

We operate with a focus on compliance, maintenance discipline, tenant durability, vendor coordination, and responsible long-term ownership.
